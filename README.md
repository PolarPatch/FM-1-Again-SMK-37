# FM-1 Again SMK-37

[FM-1 Again](https://github.com/PolarPatch/FM-1-Again) by PolarPatch for the **M-VAVE SMK-37** (Pro, Elite and
MKE-P37): warm, emotional, rhythmic electronic music, made again, on 37 keys, 16 pads, eight knobs, four faders and two
wheels. Grooves, chords pumping under the kick, chopped voices, builds and drops.

> **Not released yet.** The port starts when fm1-base's SMK-37 board option (its `smk37-inhouse` branch) is in its main
> branch. Until then: [FM-1 Again for the FM-1](https://github.com/PolarPatch/FM-1-Again) and
> [play it in your browser](https://polarpatch.github.io/FM-1-Again/).
