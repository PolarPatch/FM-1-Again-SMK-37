# FM-1 Again SMK-37 1.0.7

The first release of FM-1 Again for the M-VAVE SMK-37, on the same version line as FM-1 Again 1.0.7 for the FM-1.
**An early version:** the FM-1's controls on the SMK-37 (the middle 27 keys, the buttons and the knobs). The pads,
faders and wheels come in a later release.

- **Ableton Link over Wi-Fi:** tempo, beat and start / stop shared with Ableton Note, Live and other Link apps. WI-FI
  AUTO joins your network; AP makes the SMK-37 its own network.
- **MIDI over Wi-Fi (NETMIDI) and Bluetooth MIDI:** what you play reaches apps such as Ableton Note and GarageBand.
- **The LINK page:** GLOBE a fifth time. Press BT (SAVE) to keep WI-FI, LINK and NETMIDI over a power cycle.
- Packages: `again-smk37-1.0.7.fwsc` for the SMK-37 Pro, `again-smk37-mke-1.0.7.fwsc` for the MKE-P37. The Elite
  comes later.
- No screensaver film on the SMK-37 yet.

Tested on an SMK-37 Pro with an iPhone and Ableton Note: notes over Bluetooth and over Wi-Fi alone, Link's tempo both
ways, everything back by itself after a power cycle.
