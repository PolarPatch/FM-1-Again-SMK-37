FM-1AGAIN

Warm, emotional, rhythmic electronic music, made again on the FM-1. Press PLAY and a garage groove starts with chords pumping over it. Play soft electric piano, sung syllables or a chopped recording on the keys, hold a black key for a stutter, then press ARP and let the drop fall. Play it here: the panel below runs the same code as the instrument.

01

CONTROLS

Click the panel, or use the computer's keyboard. Drag or scroll a knob to turn it; Shift-click or right-click a key or a button to hold it down.

KEYS

Q–I A–K
The white keys, F3 up. They play what ALGORITHM chose: KEYS, VOX, CHOP, RISE or DRUMS.
1–0 -
The black keys: eleven pads, a trick each while held (SETUP › BLACK: NOTES makes them notes).
hold SEL + a white key
Any of the thirteen tricks, several at once.
Z X OCT− OCT+
The octave, −2 to +2.

BUTTONS

P PLAY
Starts and stops the groove.
ARP
BUILD at the next bar, then DROP. Again during the build: the drop at the next bar.
Esc HOME · SEQ · EDIT · ENV · LFO · FX · GLO
The pages. The same button again: its next page (SEQ: GROOVE, RISE, BUILD; ENV: VOX, CHOP; FX: SPACE, ECHO; GLO: SETUP, TAPE, MIX).
SAVE
Keeps every value, lit when something changed.
O REC
Looks for a Bluetooth MIDI device. Held a second: Bluetooth on or off.

KNOBS

K1–K4
The four values along the bottom of the screen, on every page. The one turned is lit.
PRESET
The page's main choice, in the header's second line: the groove on HOME.
ALGORITHM
What the keys play.
↑ ↓ SELECT
Steps through the pages.
MASTER
The volume.
02

SOUNDS

Five things the keys can play, all made in the firmware: no instrument's patches, no artist's recordings. Turn ALGORITHM to choose.

KEYS

EDITONE KEY, A CHORD

A soft FM electric piano of its own: a bark that softens as the note rings, a short tine at the start, and a tremolo that pans. One key plays a whole minor-ninth chord; the groove's chords play it by themselves.

TONE · DECAY · CHORDS · PLAYS

VOX

ENVSUNG SYLLABLES

A short syllable on each key, gliding from one vowel to the next, scooped up into its pitch. A run over the keys sounds like a voice cut into pieces.

VOWELS · GATE · SHIFT · BREATH

CHOP

ENV TWICEYOUR SAMPLE

A recording up to 8 s cut into slices over the keys, or one slice in pitch. Turn PRESET and the slice you just played runs backwards: a swell into its attack. Send your own from the editor.

REVERSE · SLICE · LENGTH · PITCH · MODE

RISE

SEQ TWICEA REVERSED STAB

Made forwards: its pitch climbs, it swells with a suck of noise, and it stops dead, exactly on a beat. On AUTO the groove fires one into every drop.

LENGTH · BRIGHT · SUCK · AUTO

DRUMS

ALGORITHMALL SYNTHESIZED

A kick, a clap, a ghost snare, closed and open hats, a shaker and a rim, on the keys too.

KICK · HATS · CLAP (MIX)
03

GROOVE

Eight grooves with their own swing, four chord progressions in any key, builds that drop on the bar, and a song mode that plays a whole track by itself.

GROOVES

PRESET8 OF THEM

Some hits come only half the time or every other bar, so a groove never repeats quite the same. VARY leaves hits out and adds ghosts.

SWING · VARY · KICK · HATS

CHORDS

EDITIN YOUR KEY

LONGING, NIGHT BUS, HOPE, FLOAT and FOG: four chords of two bars each, in the KEY you set. OFF leaves the keys to you.

BUILD · DROP

ARP2, 4 OR 8 BARS

The snares roll faster, the sound thins out, a riser climbs; then it drops. Four styles (SNARE, KICK, STUTTER, MIX), and RUSH lifts the tempo into the drop.

BUILD · STYLE · RUSH · PLAY

SONG

SEQ ×372 BARS

Intro, groove, build, drop, break, build, drop, outro, round and round. HOME names the section and its bar.

05

MIX

The pump that makes it breathe, and three effects from Arctic Scream: a long room, a tape echo and a worn tape over everything.

PUMP

LFOSIDECHAIN

Everything but the kick and the hats ducks under each kick, or each beat, and swells back up.

DEPTH · TIME · SHAPE · SOURCE

SPACE

FXFROM FELUCCA

A long, smooth reverb, with a shimmer above it.

SIZE · DAMP · SPACE · SHINE

ECHO

FX TWICETAPE · PING-PONG · REVERSE

A tape echo in steps of the tempo. REVERSE plays what you just played backwards, one TIME later.

TIME · AGAIN · WEAR · ECHO

TAPE

GLO TWICE5 TAPES

CLEAN, WARM, CASSETTE, WORN, MEMORY: wow, warmth, a duller top and hiss.

WOW · WARMTH · DULL · HISS

SCREENSAVER

SETUPAFTER A MINUTE

Leave it a minute and a crowd at a concert dances over the screen, dithered black and white, while the music plays on. The first touch only wakes it.

INSTALL

In Chrome or Edge, the FM-1 on USB: the installer. It can also put M-VAVE's own firmware back. Or from a terminal, with the package from the releases:

pip install mido python-rtmidi
python3 fm1_install.py again-{{VERSION}}.fwsc

Stuck? The Rescue page on the FM-1 Firmware Hub brings it back.
