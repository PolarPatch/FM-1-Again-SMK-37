# Media (not under the GPL)

`again_saver.bin` is the screensaver's film: a short piece of concert footage (the audience) from the USB002 tour's
creative files, which its team shared with the community on 27 April 2026 ("anyone in the community can now download,
edit, and use the content however they want",
<https://blog.dropbox.com/topics/news/fred-again-drops-exclusive-creative-files-from-his-usb002-tour>). It was cut,
dithered to black and white and coded by `app/tools/again_saver.py` (GPL-3.0-or-later). The film itself is the drop's
material, used as its terms allow; it is not under the GPL and it is not this project's branding. FM-1 Again is not
made, endorsed or approved by any artist.
